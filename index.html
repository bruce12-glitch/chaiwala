01 — THE RITUAL

Chai is not a drink. It is a ninety-second ceremony performed four million times a day.

/ 01

The Kettle

A battered aluminium kettle, dented on the left side by a scooter in 1991. It has never been replaced. The dent, we have decided, is part of the recipe.

VESSEL
Aluminium, 9 litres
FUEL
Kerosene, then coal
/ 02

The Flame

Full roar, never a simmer. Milk goes in cold and is beaten upward until it climbs the side of the kettle twice — that is how you know the fat has opened.

FLAME
High, 90 seconds
MILK
Full-fat, reduced 40%
/ 03

The Pull

Poured from a height of one arm, back and forth, until a crown of foam sits on the surface like a folded sheet. Aerated. Alive. Never stirred.

HEIGHT
60 cm, one arm
PULLS
Seven, then serve

03 — THE CRAFT

Four moves.
No shortcuts.

There is no machine in this building. There is a flame, a kettle, a piece of stone for crushing, and a pair of arms that have done it a few million times.

90s
  1. 01

    Source

    Second-flush Assam leaf, bought at auction in Guwahati every June. Strong, malty, unafraid of milk.

  2. 02

    Bruise

    Spice is crushed on stone, never ground to powder. A cracked cardamom pod releases oil for hours; a powdered one for minutes.

  3. 03

    Boil

    Water first, leaf second, spice third, milk last. Ninety seconds at full roar. Not eighty. Not a hundred.

  4. 04

    Pull

    Poured from height seven times to fold air into the body. The foam should hold a spoon for three seconds.
